# Deep Focus product direction v1

Approved by Timeo Williams on November 6, 2026. This is the canonical product direction for Deep Focus across desktop, mobile, and future research integrations.

## Purpose

Help a person understand where their attention and time went, how the day differed from their intentions, and what advanced through their own work and their work with agents. Support an embodied life that includes family, prayer, reading, walking, recovery, and leisure. Those activities deserve accurate documentation without being scored as failed work.

The product must also support an afternoon reading on a beach with minimal phone interaction: quiet capture where available, a brief retrospective confirmation later, and honest gaps where evidence is absent.

## The four approved directions

1. **Desktop foundation.** Verify real foreground application and browser tracking, accumulate durations, and build an editable timeline. Start with a small native macOS app. Keep collectors distinct from the shared time and evidence model so future Windows support remains possible.
2. **Proactive reconstruction.** Compare observed activity with calendar intentions. Ask about meaningful gaps when the person returns or reviews the day. Let them confirm, correct, defer, or leave an interval unknown. Respect quiet periods and avoid interrupting every half hour simply because a timer elapsed.
3. **Minimal mobile companion.** Provide quick activity tags, optional place visits, quiet periods, and offline capture. Location supplies context; it does not establish the activity. Supported collection and explicit sharing are preferred over dependence on private Find My data. The phone should add coverage without demanding continuous interaction.
4. **Research additions.** Incorporate agent-task events, then optional camera signals and eventually EEG when justified. Each addition must demonstrate useful information beyond simpler signals. Observed posture, gaze, or physiological features must not be represented as a proven measure of concentration, mental state, or the best next task without validation.

## Measurement principles

- Keep **intention**, **observation**, **inference**, and **user confirmation** distinguishable. Calendar entries are intentions; an open application is an observation, not proof of sustained attention or valuable output.
- Record the source and time interval of evidence. Corrections must preserve the distinction between the original observation and the person's interpretation.
- Keep **human attention**, **agent execution**, and **wall-clock elapsed time** separate. Concurrent agent runs must not inflate human hours. Planning, delegation, review, approval, communication, and direct execution can all be meaningful human work.
- Assess agent-assisted work through accepted results, correction/rework, elapsed time, and human effort; raw approval counts or screen time alone do not establish usefulness.
- Unknown time remains unknown until supported or confirmed. Lack of keyboard input can mean reading or thinking. Being at the gym does not prove a workout.
- Present the day in understandable, editable intervals. Collect transitions at the resolution needed for honest durations; avoid false minute-level precision in uncertain location or retrospective evidence.
- Capture only the information needed for the feature. Make optional location, camera, and physiological collection understandable and controllable. Support pause and quiet periods. Expensive remote image analysis is not a prerequisite for the core product.

## Small start and expansion

Finish and verify the desktop foundation before calling real-world tracking proven. Add calendar comparison, mobile evidence, and research integrations incrementally. Keep the core app small and its data model extensible; do not turn every future capability into a dependency of the first useful release.

## Amendment rule

This version is frozen. Do not revise, replace, or silently reinterpret its approved commitments in place. A change in product direction requires explicit operator approval and a new dated version or amendment referencing this version. Preserve this version and its checksum permanently.

Implementation details, backlog priorities, validation results, and delivery status belong in separate, editable documents. They do not amend this charter. The checksum and read-only file mode guard against accidental changes; they are not a tamper-proof archive or a remote backup.
